Languages

ViewLingo supports languages available in both Apple text recognition (OCR) and Translation on your Mac. Available languages depend on your macOS version.

ViewLingo walks you through downloading each language pack once (you can also manage them in System Settings › General › Language & Region › Translation Languages). After that, translation runs entirely offline.

To see the exact list for your Mac, open ViewLingo's settings — it reads the languages from macOS directly.

What it does

Viewfinder Translation

Press fn + Control + Option and drag over any text on your screen: games, videos, websites, documents or apps. Move or resize the viewfinder and the translation follows.

Natural AR Overlay

Translations are drawn over the original text, fitted to its layout and colors. No copying, no switching between apps.

Languages supported on your Mac

Every language that both your Mac's text recognition and Apple Translation support, so the list grows with macOS.

Fast Translation

No round-trips to a server. Translation runs locally on your Mac — without sending your screen content to a server.

Live Translation Mode

Follows changing text in video subtitles, games, slides and live streams, and holds steady when the screen doesn't change.

Vertical Text Support

Recognizes and translates vertical Japanese, Chinese and Korean text, from manga panels to signs.

Adaptive Font Sizing

Translations automatically adjust font size for optimal readability, even when translated text is longer than the original.

Controls That Stay Clear

Live, Retry and Close sit just outside your selection, so they never cover the text you're reading.
