Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Separate optional website analytics

Only after a separate Analytics choice, Google Analytics 4 measures page views after consent, landing arrivals and App Store button clicks with fixed public source, medium, campaign, page-path and CTA-position fields. Google may use first-party cookies and process IP, browser and network information; its SDK may also generate basic session and engagement events. We do not send entered text, translations, email, customer IDs or arbitrary URL queries. Clicks have value zero and are not purchases.

This purpose is independent of Google Ads and Yahoo measurement. An earlier ad grant never grants Analytics. Refusal, GPC/DNT and withdrawal prevent future Analytics collection; change your choice using “Analytics settings” in the footer. The choice and Analytics cookies last up to 60 days without extending cookie expiry on each visit. User/event-level retention is set to 2 months without activity-based reset; aggregate reports may remain longer. Enhanced measurement, Google Signals, detailed location/device collection and advertising personalization are disabled. No User-ID, customer-data matching, cross-device feature or advertising-account link is added.

Counts cover consenting activity, not all visitors or unique buyers. Each document sends one page view after consent, including internal navigation. Internal navigation within ViewLingo is not a new arrival; reloads, missing referrers and repeat visits can affect counts. App Store purchases are not joined to individual website visitors. Apple campaign reports have delays and privacy thresholds; missing data is not zero.

Website advertising measurement

The sections below describe the ViewLingo Mac app. Website measurement is optional and separate: Google Ads measures visits and App Store button clicks; Yahoo Ads (LINE Yahoo) measures App Store button clicks for registered Yahoo campaigns. Only after you explicitly allow both providers do their scripts load. They may use cookies and receive page and referring-page URLs, campaign/advertising identifiers, IP address and browser/network information. We send a zero-value Store movement, not a purchase, and do not send app screens, OCR, translation content, entered text, email, phone or customer/transaction identifiers. Advanced matching is not used; Google ad personalization is disabled and no Yahoo retargeting event is added. The Yahoo loader is skipped if the URL or referrer could contain arbitrary input. Yahoo’s optional localStorage copy is disabled. A previous Google-only grant does not grant Yahoo permission: a fresh choice is required. A previous refusal is retained. Your choice is stored locally for 180 days; browser privacy signals override permission. Declining keeps all Store links usable. Change or withdraw consent using “Ad measurement settings” in the footer; withdrawal unloads tags. Click blocking, refusal, browser restrictions and attribution windows can leave measurements incomplete. This website does not confirm App Store purchases: downloads, purchases and revenue must be reviewed separately in App Store Connect.

Yahoo landing arrivals are an additional, separate purpose. After permitting the existing measurement, you can separately allow or decline Yahoo campaign landing-arrival events. An earlier Store-click grant never grants visit measurement. These zero-value visits are not purchases. Yahoo may receive the same campaign/advertising identifiers, IP address and browser/network information. The extra choice is stored locally for 180 days, contains no customer identifier, and can be changed or withdrawn in the footer settings without changing your Store-click choice. Refusal and browser privacy signals suppress the additional events. Counts cover eligible consented page arrivals, not all visitors or unique people.

🔒 Local by default. External translation is your choice.

Regular captures and Live translation use Apple on your Mac. ViewLingo 2.x remains local. The optional DeepL connection described below is available in version 3.0; it is disabled until you set it up and consent.

Optional OpenAI comparison with your own API key in 3.2

OpenAI comparison is optional. In supported App Store regions, it is disabled until you save your own OpenAI API key and consent. The key is stored separately from DeepL in macOS Keychain. It uses your API account, not your ChatGPT subscription. Sign in with ChatGPT is not offered without commercial authorization.

Only after you press Translate with OpenAI, the selected original text, source and target languages, up to five selected preceding passages from the same capture and up to five selected terms are sent directly to OpenAI over HTTPS. Your API key authenticates the request; OpenAI also receives network information needed to deliver it. Screenshots, window titles, other history and unrelated projects are never sent. Regular Apple translation and Live never switch to OpenAI automatically.

We request store:false. This is not a guarantee of zero retention: OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs under its API policy. API data is not used for training by default unless you opt in with OpenAI. Your provider account terms, retention settings and API charges apply separately from ViewLingo.

Your App Store region is checked automatically against a local allow-list, without asking you to select a country or confirm it each session. Storefront does not prove physical location. Unverified or unsupported Storefronts, including mainland China, cannot send OpenAI requests. OpenAI may also refuse access from the actual connection location; provider refusals stop the connection. We do not collect GPS data or contact an IP-geolocation service.

You can revoke consent and delete the key even in an unsupported region. Cancellation cannot recall an already sent request. The local monthly token budget conservatively reserves input and output allowance and records usage; it is not a monetary or account-wide spending limit. Deleting local data does not delete information already processed by OpenAI.

Optional free OpenAI trial (not yet available)

We are preparing a separate, developer-funded trial. It is unavailable until its service is enabled. If offered, you may explicitly choose it instead of your own API key and separately consent to sending text. Up to 10 new comparison requests per day are counted locally on this Mac, resetting at 00:00 UTC. This is a soft limit, not an account-wide entitlement. Cached results do not count; cancellations or lost replies after dispatch may count. The trial may change, pause or end. It does not automatically switch to your paid API key.

For the trial, the previewed original text, languages, up to five selected context passages and five selected terms pass through a ViewLingo relay hosted by Cloudflare to OpenAI. The developer’s API key remains on that server; your own API key is not sent to the relay. No screenshots, window titles, app account or persistent installation identifier are sent. Apple and Live translation remain local.

The relay processes text in memory and does not intentionally log or persist translation content. Cloudflare and OpenAI process network metadata needed to provide and protect the service. Cloudflare’s edge country information is used to block unsupported or unknown locations; a client-declared country is not trusted. This is not a promise of complete anonymity or zero provider retention. OpenAI’s API retention and default no-training policy described above still apply. Aggregate project cost monitoring is separate from app analytics; no user behavior tracking is added.

Trial consent and the daily request record stay locally and are separate from BYOK consent, keys and usage. You can revoke trial permission without deleting your own key. Trial API fees are paid by the developer; your own API-key charges remain your responsibility.

If the trial is unavailable, ViewLingo shows the existing Apple translation when available. You can resume the trial manually. You can also withdraw trial consent in connection settings after the service is removed.

Default Apple translation

Apple text recognition processes captured images on your Mac. Apple translation works offline after the required language packs are downloaded. Regular capture and Live never switch to DeepL automatically. Default Apple translation does not send screenshots or translation requests to a developer server. The optional trial relay described above is a separate, consented path. The Mac app has no analytics or tracking.

Optional DeepL comparison in 3.0

DeepL requires your own API account and an internet connection. You must save a key, allow text sharing, review the preview, and press Translate with DeepL. Opening settings, editing a project or opening the comparison window does not send text. The request goes directly to DeepL over HTTPS, with your API key for authentication and the network information needed to deliver it.

  • The selected original text and its source and target languages
  • Up to five previous passages from the same capture, only when you enable and select them
  • Up to five glossary terms that you select for this request
  • Never screenshots, window titles, the full history or unrelated projects

DeepL account and data terms

DeepL API Free / Developer may store content and use it for model improvement. Do not send personal or confidential text with this plan. DeepL’s current API Pro / Growth terms exclude model training but allow limited debugging retention. Provider terms, limits and charges apply separately from ViewLingo. Review the current policies before enabling the connection.

DeepL Privacy Policy · DeepL API Terms

What stays on your Mac

Captured images, translation history, projects and glossary terms are stored locally in the app’s storage. Your API key is stored in macOS Keychain. The app keeps a local monthly usage record to enforce your ViewLingo character budget. It is not your DeepL account’s usage limit and does not include other apps. We do not collect app telemetry.

Your controls

You choose which text and options to send on each request. Context starts off. You can cancel, revoke consent, disconnect and delete saved keys. A request already sent cannot be recalled and may count toward API usage even after cancellation or a timeout. You can delete local history or projects in the app; this does not erase data already processed by DeepL. Contact DeepL about its retained data and your rights under its policy.

Screen Recording permission

macOS grants Screen Recording permission to the app so it can capture the area you select. Recognition of the image stays on your Mac. The optional external comparison sends text only. We cannot remotely access your screen through this permission.

Policy updates

We update this page when the app’s behavior or relevant requirements change. Existing 2.x versions do not gain an external translation connection from this policy update. Questions about ViewLingo’s handling of data can be sent to the contact below.

Reports you choose to send

In versions that offer Report a Problem, you can review a report on your Mac, remove optional fields, and cancel. The app version and build are included. You can include the macOS version, processor family, error code, language pair, mode and approximate processing times. Your description is optional. Only passages and images you explicitly pick from history are attached. You can cover parts of an image and review the result. Screenshots, recognized text, translations and internal logs are excluded by default.

Saving a file keeps it at the location you choose. Opening a mail draft does not send it. If you send it to admin@foundby.kr, the developer can see your sender address and the contents you chose. Your mail provider may retain delivery records. Access to received reports is limited to the developer for investigating the problem. Mail and attachments are deleted when the problem is resolved or within 90 days, whichever comes first. Email the same address to request immediate deletion. Only with your consent may a redacted copy be placed in a repository or issue.
