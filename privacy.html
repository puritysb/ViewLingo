Website advertising measurement

The sections below describe the ViewLingo Mac app. Website measurement is optional and separate: Google Ads measures visits and App Store button clicks; Yahoo Ads (LINE Yahoo) measures App Store button clicks for registered Yahoo campaigns. Only after you explicitly allow both providers do their scripts load. They may use cookies and receive page and referring-page URLs, campaign/advertising identifiers, IP address and browser/network information. We send a zero-value Store movement, not a purchase, and do not send app screens, OCR, translation content, entered text, email, phone or customer/transaction identifiers. Advanced matching is not used; Google ad personalization is disabled and no Yahoo retargeting event is added. The Yahoo loader is skipped if the URL or referrer could contain arbitrary input. Yahoo’s optional localStorage copy is disabled. A previous Google-only grant does not grant Yahoo permission: a fresh choice is required. A previous refusal is retained. Your choice is stored locally for 180 days; browser privacy signals override permission. Declining keeps all Store links usable. Change or withdraw consent using “Ad measurement settings” in the footer; withdrawal unloads tags. Click blocking, refusal, browser restrictions and attribution windows can leave measurements incomplete. This website does not confirm App Store purchases: downloads, purchases and revenue must be reviewed separately in App Store Connect.

🔒 Local by default. External translation is your choice.

Regular captures and Live translation use Apple on your Mac. ViewLingo 2.x remains local. The optional DeepL connection described below applies to version 3.0 when available; it is disabled until you set it up and consent. This policy does not announce that 3.0 is already released.

Default Apple translation

Apple text recognition processes captured images on your Mac. Apple translation works offline after the required language packs are downloaded. Regular capture and Live never switch to DeepL automatically. We do not operate a server that receives your app screenshots or translation requests, and the Mac app has no analytics or tracking.

Optional DeepL comparison in 3.0

DeepL requires your own API account and an internet connection. You must save a key, allow text sharing, review the preview, and press Translate with DeepL. Opening settings, editing a project or opening the comparison window does not send text. The request goes directly to DeepL over HTTPS, with your API key for authentication and the network information needed to deliver it.

  • The selected original text and its source and target languages
  • Up to five previous passages from the same capture, only when you enable and select them
  • Up to five glossary terms that you select for this request
  • Never screenshots, window titles, the full history or unrelated projects

DeepL account and data terms

DeepL API Free / Developer may store content and use it for model improvement. Do not send personal or confidential text with this plan. DeepL’s current API Pro / Growth terms exclude model training but allow limited debugging retention. Provider terms, limits and charges apply separately from ViewLingo. Review the current policies before enabling the connection.

What stays on your Mac

Captured images, translation history, projects and glossary terms are stored locally in the app’s storage. Your API key is stored in macOS Keychain. The app keeps a local monthly usage record to enforce your ViewLingo character budget. It is not your DeepL account’s usage limit and does not include other apps. We do not collect app telemetry.

Your controls

You choose which text and options to send on each request. Context starts off. You can cancel, revoke consent, disconnect and delete saved keys. A request already sent cannot be recalled and may count toward API usage even after cancellation or a timeout. You can delete local history or projects in the app; this does not erase data already processed by DeepL. Contact DeepL about its retained data and your rights under its policy.

Screen Recording permission

macOS grants Screen Recording permission to the app so it can capture the area you select. Recognition of the image stays on your Mac. The optional external comparison sends text only. We cannot remotely access your screen through this permission.

Policy updates

We update this page when the app’s behavior or relevant requirements change. Existing 2.x versions do not gain an external translation connection from this policy update. Questions about ViewLingo’s handling of data can be sent to the contact below.
