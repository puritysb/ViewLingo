# ViewLingo Privacy Policy

🌍 Language: **English** | [한국어](docs/PRIVACY-ko.md) | [日本語](https://puritysb.github.io/ViewLingo/ja/privacy.html) | [简体中文](https://puritysb.github.io/ViewLingo/zh-hans/privacy.html) | [繁體中文](https://puritysb.github.io/ViewLingo/zh-hant/privacy.html)

- ViewLingo recognizes text on your Mac. Apple translation is the default for captures and the engine for Live and Shortcuts. Screenshots and translation history remain on your Mac.
- Regular captures use a connected external engine only after you separately opt in. The selected service receives recognized text and the source and target languages. Your API keys are stored in macOS Keychain.
- The optional OpenAI trial sends text through the ViewLingo relay. Comparisons and trial captures share a daily allowance. Captures use Apple translation when the selected service or allowance is unavailable.
- Manual comparisons send only what you select. When available, Apple Private Cloud Compute is an optional comparison service with separate consent and an Apple service quota.
- You can withdraw permission in the app. The full policy describes what each service receives, third-party policies, and separate measurement choices on the website.
